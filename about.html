My research integrates experimental ecology, biogeochemistry, and multi-omics with quantitative and bioinformatic approaches to investigate the mechanisms underlying ecosystem processes across natural and built environments. I value multidisciplinary collaboration, experiential learning, and effective scientific communication through writing, data visualization, presentations, and outreach. Central to my approach is fostering a supportive research culture where curiosity, scientific rigor, respect, and kindness advance scientific discovery.
