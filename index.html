ABOUT

Understanding the invisible processes that shape ecosystems.

I am a microbial ecologist and environmental scientist studying how microbial communities mediate biogeochemical transformations and respond to environmental change.

My research integrates microbial genomics, environmental microbiology, and biogeochemistry across coastal sediments, soils, deep-sea environments, and the built environment.

Current affiliation: University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, Institute of Marine Sciences.
